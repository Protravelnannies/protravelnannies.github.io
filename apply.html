Nanny application

Come and join us

Tell us a bit about yourself, your experience and the bookings you'd love to do. It takes around 15 minutes, and a real person reads it at the other end.

What happens next

  1. We review your application, usually within a few working days.
  2. Video interview: a relaxed chat about your experience, nothing scary.
  3. Identity, documents & references: we speak to your referees ourselves.
  4. Sex-offence certificate: everyone who works with children needs one.
  5. Your profile goes live with your photo, intro video and badges.

No need to upload any documents yet. We'll ask when we get to that point.

Want to ask something first? Email cameron@protravelnannies.com or read the nanny overview.
