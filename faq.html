FAQs

Your questions, answered honestly.

Whether you're sorting childcare for a trip or thinking about nannying with us, these are the things people ask me most. If yours isn't here, just send me a message.

Questions from families

How do you vet your nannies?

Each nanny fills in a detailed application, verifies their ID, does a video interview with us and gives references, which we phone or email ourselves. If they have first aid or other qualifications, we check those and put them on their profile. We also check their Spanish sex-offence certificate, which the law requires for anyone working with children. Profiles show a separate badge for each check, so you can see exactly what's been done and what it means.

What each badge means

Can I meet or speak to my nanny before the booking?

Yes. Before you accept a match you'll see your nanny's profile, photo and intro video. Once the booking's confirmed, we'll swap contact details so you can say hi, send arrival instructions and ask anything you like before the day.

Do your nannies speak English, or other languages?

Yes, and lots of them speak other languages as well. You can ask for a nanny who speaks Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and more. Profiles show each nanny's English level and the other languages they speak. If your kids speak another language at home, let us know and we'll keep that in mind when matching.

What ages do you care for?

All ages, from babies up to older kids. Each profile shows which age groups the nanny has experience with, and we'll only suggest someone whose experience fits your children. We also have specialist newborn and infant care.

Where does the childcare take place?

Wherever you're staying or celebrating. That might be a hotel, villa, holiday apartment, your own home or a wedding venue. Travel nannies can also come with you on flights, transfers and day trips. We're based in Spain, with our home network on the Costa del Sol, Málaga, the Balearic Islands and Spain's major cities, and we look after families travelling across Europe.

See our destinations

Can the nanny supervise swimming?

When you send your request, tell us if your kids can swim, whether it's a pool or the beach, and if you're expecting them to swim. We'll match you with a nanny who's done this before. Profiles show the difference between things a nanny says about themselves, like "confident swimmer", and qualifications we've checked, like water-safety training.

Can the nanny drive my children?

We never assume driving, so please mention it in your request. If it's agreed, we'll make sure the nanny has a valid licence and the right insurance, and that there are proper child seats. No nanny should turn up and only then find out they're expected to drive.

What if my child is unwell?

If your child has vomiting, diarrhoea, a fever or anything contagious before the booking, please tell us as soon as you can. We'll work out what to do with you and the nanny. If they get poorly during a booking, your nanny will call you straight away and follow our procedures.

Read our sick child policy

What is your cancellation policy?

If you cancel well in advance, it's free or costs very little. The charge goes up as the date gets closer and is highest for same-day cancellations, because your nanny has kept that time for you. Holiday and travel nanny bookings have their own terms. You'll see the exact terms for your booking before you pay.

Booking & cancellation policies

How do I pay?

You pay securely through us, not to the nanny. Before you pay you'll see the full breakdown, travel costs and extras included. Your booking's confirmed once we've received payment.

See pricing

What happens if my nanny can't make it?

If your nanny has a real emergency, we'll tell you straight away and do all we reasonably can to find a suitable replacement, as close a match to your original booking as we can get.

What if something goes wrong during a booking?

Get in touch. There's a real person on hand while your booking is running. In an emergency your nanny follows a set order: keep your child safe, call emergency services if needed, then call you, then us. For anything else, we'll hear from both you and your nanny and look at what happened fairly.

Trust & safety

Can I book the same nanny again?

Of course, and I hope you will. We already know your family, so to rebook a nanny you loved we just need the new dates, hours, location and anything that's changed. If they're busy, we'll suggest someone else who'd suit you just as well.

Can I book at the last minute?

We'll always try. Finding someone within 12 to 24 hours takes a lot more work to do properly, so a last-minute fee may apply, and you'll see it before you pay. The earlier you book, the more nannies you'll have to choose from, especially in summer.

Why do you verify families too?

Because nannies want to feel sure about you too. Trust goes both ways. Checking families means nannies get accurate information about the children and the booking, and that makes for better care and better matches.

Questions from nannies

What do I need to join?

Real childcare experience that we can check, a professional and caring way with kids, good English, and references we can contact. Nannies, experienced babysitters, au pairs, early-years workers and teachers are all welcome. I care more about what you've actually done with children than your job title.

Do I need perfect English?

No. Your profile shows your English level honestly, and plenty of brilliant nannies don't speak it perfectly. What matters is that you can talk clearly and confidently with parents and kids, especially about safety. Other languages are a big plus.

How do I apply?

Fill in the online application with your experience, the ages you've looked after, languages, qualifications, skills, availability and references. We read each application ourselves and will get back to you about next steps.

Apply now

What does the vetting process involve?

Once you're through the first screening, we verify your ID, do a video interview, check your references, first aid and qualifications, and check your Spanish sex-offence certificate (plus the equivalent from your home country if you're not Spanish). Then we put your profile and intro video together with you.

How long does vetting take?

Mostly it depends on how quickly your referees reply and how fast your documents and checks come back. We'll let you know where things are at each stage. Having your documents and referee details ready speeds it up a lot.

What documents will I need?

Usually a government photo ID, right-to-work documents if they apply to you, your Spanish sex-offence certificate (a legal requirement for working with children), certificates for first aid and any qualifications, and contact details for your referees. We'll tell you exactly what you need for your situation. Your documents stay private and never appear on your profile.

How much will I earn, and when am I paid?

You'll see what you'll earn for a booking before you accept it, along with the hours, what's expected of you and any travel plans. We pay you once the booking's finished and any changes, like extra hours, are confirmed. You'll never have to chase a family for money.

Am I paid for extra hours?

Yes. If a family asks you to stay on and you say yes, we record the extra time and pay you for it. You never have to stay past the agreed finish time.

What happens if a family cancels?

Once a booking's confirmed, that time is yours. The cancellation charges go up as the date gets closer, because you've kept those dates free, and longer holiday and travel bookings have their own rules. You'll see the cancellation terms before you accept anything.

Can I choose which bookings I accept?

Yes. Tell us the ages, number of children, locations, availability and types of booking you prefer, and we'll only send you matches that suit your experience. You'll see the family profile and full brief first, and you can always say no.

Are families verified?

Yes. Families verify their identity, contact details and payment, give us accurate details about their children and confirm a booking brief. You'll see which checks they've completed before you accept.

Who pays my travel costs?

Reasonable costs the booking needs, like a late-night taxi, a long journey, or flights and accommodation on a travel nanny booking, are agreed before it's confirmed. They shouldn't come out of your pocket.

What if a child is sick when I arrive?

We ask families to tell us about illness before the booking. Nobody should pressure you into care that's outside the agreed booking or puts your health at unreasonable risk. Get in touch with us and we'll help you sort it out.

Sick child policy

What if I feel unsafe or something goes wrong?

Get in touch with us. You're not on your own. If the booking information was wrong, the place doesn't feel safe or someone's behaving inappropriately, we'll listen, look at what happened and treat you fairly.

What's it like being part of the network?

You get a professional profile that shows off your skills, work with international families, clear information on each booking and backup from people who've done the job themselves. Later on we're planning to add things to help you grow, like a childcare English toolkit and training.
