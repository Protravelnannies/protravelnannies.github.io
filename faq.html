FAQs

Good questions, honest answers.

Whether you're planning childcare for a trip in Europe or thinking about joining our nanny network, here are the questions we're asked most. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Questions from families

How do you vet your nannies?

Every nanny completes a structured application, identity verification, a video interview and reference checks, where we contact referees directly. We verify first aid and relevant qualifications, and complete background checks where legally appropriate and available. Each profile shows individual badges, so you can see exactly which checks have been completed and what each one means.

What each badge means

Can I meet or speak to my nanny before the booking?

Yes. Before you accept a match you'll see your nanny's profile, photo and introduction video. Once your booking is confirmed, we share contact details so you can say hello, share arrival instructions and ask any questions before the day.

Do your nannies speak English, or other languages?

Yes, and many speak other languages too. Alongside English, families can ask for nannies who speak Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and more. Each profile shows the nanny's English level and every other language they speak, so if your children speak another language at home, tell us and we'll match with that in mind.

What ages do you care for?

We match nannies to children of all ages, from babies to older children. Every nanny's profile shows their experience by age group, and we only propose nannies whose experience fits your children. Dedicated newborn and infant specialist care is coming soon.

Where does the childcare take place?

Wherever you're staying or celebrating: hotels, villas, holiday apartments, your own home, or wedding and event venues. Travel nannies can also accompany you on flights, transfers and day trips. We're based in Spain, with our home network on the Costa del Sol, Málaga, the Balearic Islands and Spain's major cities, and we care for families travelling across Europe.

See our destinations

Can the nanny supervise swimming?

Tell us in your request whether your children can swim, the pool or beach setting and whether swimming is expected. We'll match you with a nanny with relevant experience. Profiles clearly distinguish self-declared skills, like "confident swimmer", from verified qualifications such as water-safety training.

Can the nanny drive my children?

Driving is never assumed, so please mention it in your request. Where driving is agreed, we'll confirm the nanny has a valid licence and appropriate insurance, and that suitable child seats are available. A nanny should never discover after arriving that driving is expected.

What if my child is unwell?

Please let us know as soon as possible if your child has vomiting, diarrhoea, a fever or a contagious illness before the booking. We'll talk through the options with you and the nanny. If your child becomes unwell during a booking, your nanny will contact you straight away and follow our procedures.

Read our sick child policy

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancelling well in advance is free or low-cost, with charges increasing as the booking date approaches and stronger protection for same-day cancellations, because your nanny has reserved that time for you. Holiday and travel nanny bookings have their own terms. The exact terms for your booking are always shown before you pay.

Booking & cancellation policies

How do I pay?

You pay securely through us, not directly to the nanny. Before paying you'll see a full breakdown, including any travel costs or extras. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

See pricing

What happens if my nanny can't make it?

If your nanny has a genuine emergency, we'll let you know straight away and make every reasonable effort to find a suitable replacement, matched as closely as possible to your original booking.

What if something goes wrong during a booking?

Contact us. A real person is available to support you during your booking. In an emergency, your nanny follows a clear procedure: protect your child, contact emergency services if needed, then you, then us. For anything else, we review the facts fairly with both you and your nanny.

Trust & safety

Can I book the same nanny again?

Absolutely, and we hope you will. We already know your family, so rebooking a nanny you love only needs your new dates, hours, location and anything that's changed. If they're unavailable, we'll suggest another nanny who fits just as well.

Can I book at the last minute?

We'll always try. Childcare needed within 12 to 24 hours takes extra work to arrange properly, so a last-minute fee may apply, shown before you pay. Booking earlier gives you the widest choice of nannies, especially in summer.

Why do you verify families too?

Because great nannies deserve the same confidence you do. Verifying families means nannies receive accurate information about the children and the booking, which leads to better care and better matches.

Questions from nannies

What do I need to join?

Genuine, verifiable childcare experience, a professional and caring approach, good communication in English, and references we can contact. Professional nannies, experienced babysitters, au pairs, early-years professionals and teachers are all welcome. We look at your real experience and suitability, not just a job title.

Do I need perfect English?

No. Profiles show an honest English level, and an otherwise excellent nanny doesn't need perfect English. You'll need to communicate clearly and confidently with parents and children, especially about safety. Other languages are a real advantage.

How do I apply?

Complete our online application with your experience, the ages you've cared for, languages, qualifications, skills, availability and references. We review every application personally and will be in touch about next steps.

Apply now

What does the vetting process involve?

After initial screening: identity verification, a video interview, reference checks, verification of first aid and qualifications, and background checks where legally appropriate and available. Then we build your profile and introduction video together.

How long does vetting take?

It depends mainly on how quickly references respond and documents and checks come through. We'll keep you updated at each stage, and you can help by having your documents and referee details ready.

What documents will I need?

Usually a government-issued photo ID, right-to-work documentation where relevant, certificates for first aid and any qualifications, and contact details for your references. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your situation. Your documents are kept private and never shown on your profile.

How much will I earn, and when am I paid?

You'll see your expected earnings for every booking before you accept it, along with the hours, responsibilities and any travel arrangements. Payment is handled through us after the booking is completed and any changes, like extra hours, are confirmed. You'll never need to chase a family for money.

Am I paid for extra hours?

Yes. If a family asks you to stay longer and you agree, the extra time is recorded and you're paid for it. You're never obliged to stay beyond the agreed finish time.

What happens if a family cancels?

Once a booking is confirmed, your time is reserved. Our cancellation terms become stronger as the booking date approaches, to respect the dates you've kept free, with separate rules for longer holiday and travel bookings. You'll see the cancellation terms before you accept any booking.

Can I choose which bookings I accept?

Yes. You tell us your preferred ages, number of children, locations, availability and booking types, and we only send matches that suit your experience. You see the family profile and full brief first, and you can always decline.

Are families verified?

Yes. Families complete identity, contact and payment verification, plus accurate details about their children and a confirmed booking brief. You'll see which verification steps are complete before you accept.

Who pays my travel costs?

Reasonable costs needed for the booking, such as a late-night taxi, long-distance travel, or flights and accommodation for travel nanny bookings, are agreed before confirmation. You shouldn't personally absorb them.

What if a child is sick when I arrive?

Families are asked to disclose illness before the booking. You're never pressured to provide care outside the agreed booking or that creates an unreasonable health risk. Contact us and we'll support you through it.

Sick child policy

What if I feel unsafe or something goes wrong?

Contact us. You're not on your own. Whether it's incorrect booking information, an unsafe environment or inappropriate behaviour, we'll listen, review the facts and treat you fairly.

What's it like being part of the network?

You'll have a professional profile that showcases your skills, access to international families, clear booking information and support from people who've done this job. Over time we plan to add development resources, including a childcare English toolkit and training opportunities.
