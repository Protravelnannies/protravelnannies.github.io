What a day might look like

No two families do it the same way. Here's one example of how eight hours could go.

Photo: beach morning with a holiday nanny

  1. 09:00 · Morning handover

    A quick chat about the plan for the day, then breakfast and getting everyone ready for the sun.

  2. 10:00 · Beach or pool

    Sandcastles, games and someone keeping a close eye, with swimming done the way you've agreed beforehand.

  3. 12:30 · Lunch and quiet time

    Lunch, then a nap for the little ones or something quiet in the shade.

  4. 15:00 · Afternoon activity

    Crafts, a walk, the playground, or coming along on a family outing where another pair of hands helps.

  5. 17:00 · Dinner and wind-down

    Kids' dinner, bath and pyjamas, so they're ready for bed or a quiet evening.

What your booking agreement covers

Your nanny is keeping several days free just for your family, so before anything's confirmed, everyone agrees exactly what the booking involves. Trust goes both ways.

01

Working hours

The hours each day, which days, and how any extra hours work and are charged.

02

Breaks & days off

When your nanny takes a break each day, and any days off within a longer stay.

03

Accommodation

Where the care happens. If your nanny is staying nearby or with you, the sleeping arrangements too, including a private room where needed.

04

Meals

Whether your nanny eats with you, has meals provided or gets an agreed allowance.

05

Transport

How your nanny gets to you each day, any taxis or mileage, and whether they'll be doing any driving.

06

Responsibilities & changes

What's included in the role, what isn't, and how changes and cancellations work.

Questions

Holiday nanny FAQs

How many days do I need to book?

A holiday nanny is meant for several days of your stay. If you only need one evening or afternoon, babysitting probably suits you better. Tell us your plans and we'll point you the right way.

Can the hours change from day to day?

Yes, within what we've agreed. Lots of families mix daytime care with an evening or two. The schedule goes in your booking agreement, and any extra hours are charged at the agreed rate.

Does the nanny need to stay with us?

Not usually. Most holiday nannies come to you each day. If you're somewhere remote, or you'd like your nanny close by, we'll sort out accommodation and sleeping arrangements before confirming.

Can our nanny come on excursions?

Often, yes. Tell us what you're planning so transport, timings and any costs are agreed beforehand. We never assume your nanny will drive. If you'd like them to, we'll talk through licence, insurance and child seats first.

What happens if our nanny is unwell?

We'll tell you straight away and do everything we reasonably can to find a replacement who fits your original booking as closely as possible.

Can we speak to the nanny before we arrive?

Yes. You'll see their profile and introduction video before you confirm, and once it's booked we'll put you in touch so you can say hello before you travel.
