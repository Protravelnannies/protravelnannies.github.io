Clear on both sides

What your booking agreement covers

A holiday nanny reserves several days of their calendar for your family. So before anything is confirmed, everyone agrees exactly what the booking involves. Trust goes both ways.

01

Working hours

Daily hours, which days, and how additional hours are handled and charged.

02

Breaks & days off

When your nanny takes a break each day, and any days off within a longer stay.

03

Accommodation

Where care takes place and, if your nanny stays nearby or with you, the sleeping arrangements, including a private room where needed.

04

Meals

Whether your nanny eats with the family, has meals provided or receives an agreed allowance.

05

Transport

How your nanny travels to you each day, any taxis or mileage, and whether any driving is involved.

06

Responsibilities & changes

What's included in the role, what isn't, and how changes and cancellations work.

Longer bookings receive a more detailed personal review before confirmation. Read our booking and cancellation terms.

Questions

Holiday nanny FAQs

How many days do I need to book?

A holiday nanny is designed for several days of your stay. If you only need one evening or afternoon, babysitting is likely a better fit. Tell us your plans and we'll suggest what works.

Can the hours change from day to day?

Yes, within what's agreed. Many families mix daytime care with an evening or two. We'll set out the schedule in your booking agreement, and any extra hours are charged at the agreed rate.

Does the nanny need to stay with us?

Not usually. Most holiday nannies travel to you each day. If your stay is somewhere remote, or you'd like your nanny to stay nearby, we'll agree accommodation and sleeping arrangements before confirmation.

Can our nanny come on excursions?

Often, yes. Let us know your plans so transport, timings and any costs are agreed in advance. Driving is never assumed. If you'd like your nanny to drive, we'll discuss licence, insurance and child seats first.

What happens if our nanny is unwell?

We'll let you know straight away and make every reasonable effort to find a suitable replacement, matched as closely as possible to your original booking.

Can we speak to the nanny before we arrive?

Yes. You'll see their profile and introduction video before you confirm, and once your booking is confirmed we'll put you in touch so you can say hello before your trip.
