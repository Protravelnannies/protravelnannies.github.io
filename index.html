Meet the founder

“I spent years being the nanny. Now I'm building the company I wish existed.”

I'm Cameron Hein. It all started in South Africa with my nieces and nephews. From there I became an au pair in New Jersey, spent time in Costa Rica, and then worked for around four years as a nanny and babysitter for international families in Spain.

I've had the lovely bookings and the messy ones, and I've seen what goes wrong for parents and for nannies. That's why I built Pro Travel Nannies around one idea: trust goes both ways.

Our philosophy

Trust goes both ways.

Most childcare services only ask whether the family can trust the nanny. We ask the other question too: can the nanny trust the family? When both sides know what they're walking into, and both are looked after, the kids get better care.

For families

Know who's looking after your kids

  • Verified nanny profiles with photos and introduction videos
  • References we contact ourselves, not just names on a form
  • A nanny matched to your children's ages, languages and needs
  • The full price and booking terms before you pay anything
  • Someone real to message if plans change or something goes wrong
&
For nannies

Know who you're working for

  • Verified families and honest, accurate details about the children
  • Hours, duties and expectations in writing before you say yes
  • Payment goes through us, so you're never chasing it afterwards
  • Cancellation terms that protect the dates you've kept free
  • Someone to call when something unexpected comes up

How it works

We want to know your family, not just your dates

You won't just get a phone number from us. We get to know your family, find the right person and stay involved until the booking's done.

  • Verify
  • Understand
  • Match
  • Protect

See the full process

  1. Tell us about your family

    Where you're going, when, your children's ages and languages, and anything your nanny should know. It takes about five minutes.

  2. We review and match

    We read your request ourselves and match you with a verified nanny based on experience, ages, languages, location and personality.

  3. Get to know each other

    Look through your nanny's profile, checks and introduction video. Your nanny sees your family's brief too, so nobody turns up with the wrong idea.

  4. Confirm with clear terms

    Once you've both said yes and payment is in, your booking is confirmed and your nanny's calendar is held for you.

  5. Enjoy yourselves, we're still around

    Your nanny looks after the kids and we're a message away. Afterwards you both share feedback, and booking the same nanny again is easy.

Questions

Questions we get asked a lot

How do you check your nannies?

Each nanny goes through a structured application, identity verification, a video interview and reference checks, and we speak to their referees ourselves. If a nanny has first aid or other qualifications, we verify them and show them on their profile. We also check every nanny's Spanish sex-offence certificate, which the law requires for anyone working with children. You can see on each profile exactly which checks have been done.

Read about our verification process

Can I meet or speak to my nanny before the booking?

Yes. You'll see your nanny's profile and introduction video before you confirm. Once the booking's confirmed, we'll put you in touch so you can say hello before you arrive.

What happens if my nanny gets sick?

If your nanny has a real emergency, we'll tell you straight away and do everything we reasonably can to find a suitable replacement, as close a match to your original booking as possible.

Why do you verify families too?

Because good nannies should get to feel as confident as you do. When families are verified, nannies get accurate information about the children and the booking, and that makes for better matches and better care.
