Included every time

What you get with any booking

You're not paying for a name on a list. We manage the booking with you, from your first message until the kids are in bed on the last night.

I nannied for international families for years, and the bookings that went well always had the same things going for them: good information, the right match, clear terms and someone to call when plans changed. So we've built those into all our bookings.

Cameron

  • Verified nanny profile with photos, an introduction video and the specific checks completed
  • Personal matching based on your children's ages, languages, needs and personalities
  • A clear childcare brief, so your nanny arrives knowing your children, routines and expectations
  • Transparent pricing: childcare, any travel costs and any extras shown before you pay
  • Clear booking terms covering hours, responsibilities and cancellation for both sides
  • Secure payment handled through us, not cash on the night
  • Real human support before, during and after the booking
  • Easy rebooking of a nanny your family loved

How booking works

Booking works the same way, whatever you choose

Longer or trickier bookings just mean more conversation and a closer look before we confirm.

  • Verify
  • Understand
  • Match
  • Protect
  • Support
See the full process

  1. Tell us your plans

    Destination, dates, times, your children's ages and anything your nanny should know.

  2. We review and match

    We read each request ourselves and suggest a verified nanny who fits your family.

  3. Confirm with clear terms

    Hours, responsibilities, price and cancellation terms are all agreed before you pay.

  4. Enjoy your time

    Your nanny looks after the kids, and we're a message away if you need us.
