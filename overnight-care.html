Two kinds of overnight care

Sleeping or waking: what's the difference?

Being clear about this from the start means everyone knows what to expect, including your nanny.

Sleeping overnight care

Close by, if they're needed

Your nanny sleeps in the same accommodation, with their own appropriate sleeping space, and remains responsible for the children if they wake or need anything.

  • Best for children who usually sleep through
  • Ideal after a late event or before an early start
  • Occasional wake-ups are part of the role

Example: parents attend a wedding reception until 2am and stay at the venue. Your nanny settles the children at bedtime and sleeps in the villa's spare room, close enough to hear them.

&
Waking overnight care

Awake and actively caring

Your nanny stays awake and provides active care for significant portions of the night: settling, comforting and attending to children as needed.

  • Best for children who wake often or need settling
  • Helpful during adjustment to a new time zone
  • Gives parents a genuinely uninterrupted night

Example: a toddler struggling with jet lag after a long-haul flight. Your nanny is awake through the night, so the parents can sleep and start the next day rested.

Clear rules, better nights

Overnight care only works well when both sides know exactly what's been agreed. So we set it out in writing, every time.

Questions

Overnight care FAQs

Can overnight care follow a full day of childcare?

It can be combined with daytime care, but your nanny needs proper rest to care well. We'll plan the schedule so hours and rest are realistic, which may mean a second nanny for longer stretches.

Where will the nanny sleep?

For sleeping overnight care, your nanny needs a suitable, private sleeping space close enough to hear the children. We'll confirm the arrangements before booking.

Do you offer overnight care for newborns?

Newborn overnight care will be offered through our specialist newborn and infant team, which is coming soon. Register your interest and tell us about your plans.

What happens if my child is unwell in the night?

Your nanny will follow the instructions and emergency procedures agreed in advance, and contact you as agreed. Please tell us about any illness before the booking so your nanny can be prepared.

Can I book overnight care on its own?

Yes, although it's often added to a holiday nanny, travel nanny or event booking. Tell us what you need and we'll suggest the best arrangement.
