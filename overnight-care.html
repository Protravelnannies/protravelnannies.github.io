Two kinds of overnight care

Sleeping or waking: what's the difference?

Getting this clear from the start means nobody's guessing, your nanny included.

Sleeping overnight care

Close by, if they're needed

Your nanny sleeps where you're staying, in a suitable space of their own, and is still responsible for the children if they wake up or need anything.

  • Best for children who usually sleep through
  • Good after a late event or before an early start
  • Occasional wake-ups are part of the role

Example: the parents are at a wedding reception until 2am and stay over at the venue. Your nanny settles the children at bedtime and sleeps in the villa's spare room, close enough to hear them.

&
Waking overnight care

Up and looking after them

Your nanny stays awake and is actively caring for the children for much of the night, settling them, comforting them and seeing to whatever they need.

  • Best for children who wake often or need settling
  • Helpful while they get used to a new time zone
  • Gives parents an uninterrupted night

Example: a toddler who's all over the place with jet lag after a long-haul flight. Your nanny stays awake through the night, so the parents can sleep and wake up ready for the next day.

Clear rules, better nights

We agree it all before bedtime

Questions

Overnight care FAQs

Can overnight care follow a full day of childcare?

It can be combined with daytime care, but nobody looks after children well without proper rest. We'll plan a schedule with realistic hours and rest, and for longer stretches that might mean a second nanny.

Where will the nanny sleep?

For sleeping overnight care, your nanny needs a suitable private space to sleep, close enough to hear the children. We'll confirm the details before booking.

Do you offer overnight care for newborns?

Yes. Our specialist newborn and infant team look after newborns overnight. Send us a booking request and tell us what you're planning.

What happens if my child is unwell in the night?

Your nanny follows the instructions and emergency procedures agreed beforehand, and gets in touch with you the way you've arranged. If your child's been poorly, please tell us before the booking so your nanny is prepared.

Can I book overnight care on its own?

Yes, although lots of families add it to a holiday nanny, travel nanny or event booking. Tell us what you need and we'll suggest what makes sense.
