4. Eligibility & accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to request a booking, create a family or nanny profile or apply to join our network.
  • Families must be the parent or legal guardian of the children to be cared for, or be authorised by them to book childcare.
  • Nannies must have the legal right to work in Spain and must complete our verification process before accepting bookings.
  • You must give accurate information and keep it up to date, and keep any login details confidential. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • We may decline a request or application at our discretion, for example where we cannot verify information or provide a suitable match. We will not decline anyone on discriminatory grounds.

5. Family responsibilities

Good care starts with accurate information. As a family, you agree to:

  • Give accurate information about your children: number, ages, routines, behaviour and needs, and any allergies, medical conditions or medication the nanny needs to know about.
  • Disclose illness: tell us and the nanny before the booking if a child is unwell or has symptoms such as fever, vomiting, diarrhoea or a contagious illness. The nanny may decline care in some circumstances. What happens to the booking and payment is set out in our Booking & Cancellation Policies.
  • Provide a safe environment: make sure the accommodation is reasonably safe for children and the nanny, tell us about pools, balconies, pets, firearms or other hazards, and let the nanny know about safety equipment and emergency exits.
  • Be reachable: stay contactable by phone throughout the booking, give an emergency contact, and return when reasonably requested if a child becomes seriously unwell.
  • Stick to the agreed booking: do not add children, duties (such as driving, swimming supervision or housework), hours or locations without the nanny's agreement and, where applicable, an adjusted price.
  • Leave clear instructions on routines, medication (with written consent) and any rules you want followed.
  • Treat the nanny with respect: provide breaks, meals and, for overnight or travel bookings, suitable sleeping arrangements as agreed in the booking brief.

6. Nanny responsibilities

Nannies in our network agree to:

  • give accurate information about their identity, experience, qualifications and availability, and tell us promptly about any change, including anything affecting their background check;
  • put the safety and wellbeing of the children first, and follow our safeguarding and child-safety standards and the family's reasonable instructions;
  • arrive on time, prepared, and fit to work: never under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and not smoking or vaping around children;
  • not bring visitors, transport children, take them swimming or leave the agreed location unless this has been agreed with the family;
  • keep information about families and children confidential, during and after the booking;
  • follow the rules on photos, videos and social media in section 12;
  • report any incident, injury, illness or safeguarding concern to the family and to us as soon as possible; and
  • honour confirmed bookings and give as much notice as possible of a genuine emergency, so we can make every reasonable effort to find a suitable replacement.

Full details are set out in the [Nanny Agreement].
