How it works

We get to know your family before we send anyone.

You won't get a phone number and then silence. We get to know your family, find the right person, make sure everyone's agreed on the details, and stick around until the booking's finished. Nannies get the same treatment from our side.

Step by step

Are you a parent or a nanny?

Trust goes both ways, so there are two versions of this: one for families and one for nannies. Pick yours.

For families

From your first message to the last bedtime of the trip

Whether it's a babysitter for one evening in Marbella or a nanny for a fortnight in Mallorca, it works the same way. Longer, travel and event bookings just get a closer look along the way.

What you'll need to start

  • Your destination, dates and approximate times
  • Your children's ages and the languages they speak
  • Where care will happen (hotel, villa, apartment, venue)
  • Anything special: swimming, routines, allergies, events

It takes about five minutes. Don't worry if you don't have every detail yet. We'll help you fill in the brief.

Start your request

  1. Tell us about your family

    Tell us where and when, your children's ages and interests, the languages they speak, where you're staying and anything your nanny should know. This is the start of your childcare brief.

  2. We review your request personally

    A real person reads your request. We check the details, ask whatever we need to, complete your family verification and work out what kind of care fits. Some bookings need a closer look, like travel, events or very young babies.

  3. We match you with the right nanny

    Being free on your dates isn't enough. We look at your children's ages, experience, languages, location, swimming or driving needs, qualifications, personality and the nanny's own preferences. You'll know why we think each nanny is a good fit.

  4. Review profiles and accept the match

    See your nanny's photo, introduction video, experience and the checks we've done. At the same time, your nanny looks over your family profile and brief. Nothing moves forward until you've both said yes.

  5. Payment is secured and your booking is confirmed

    You'll see the full price, including any travel costs or extras, before you pay. Once payment's in and the terms are accepted, your booking is confirmed and your nanny's calendar is held for you.

  6. Contact details are shared and you say hello

    After confirmation, we share the contact details you both need, so you can say hello, sort out arrival instructions and ask any last questions before the day.

  7. Enjoy your time, with support behind you

    Your nanny looks after the kids using the brief you agreed. If plans change, your nanny is delayed or something unexpected happens, you can reach us. We're around the whole time.

  8. Share feedback and book again easily

    Afterwards, you and your nanny both share feedback. If you loved them, booking again is easy. We already know your family, so you just tell us what's changed.

For nannies

We get to know you, so we can put you forward properly

This isn't an open sign-up. We get to know everyone who joins, because that's what gives families the confidence to book you. It's also how we find you families and bookings that actually suit you.

What you can expect from us

  • Verified families and correct details about the children
  • A full childcare brief before you decide
  • Your earnings, hours and responsibilities shown up front
  • Your calendar only blocked once a booking is confirmed and paid
  • Payment handled through us, not chased afterwards
  • Someone to call if something doesn't feel right
Apply to join Why nannies join

  1. Apply online

    Tell us about your childcare experience, the ages you've worked with, languages, qualifications, skills, availability, travel preferences and references.

  2. Initial screening

    We read each application ourselves, looking at your experience, how you communicate and the kind of childcare you'd like to do.

  3. Identity verification

    We confirm your identity using appropriate documents and, where relevant, right-to-work information. These stay private and never appear on your profile.

  4. Video interview

    A friendly chat (still a professional one) about your experience, how you think about safety, and the families and bookings you'd enjoy most.

  5. Reference checks

    We get in touch with your referees ourselves to hear how reliable you are, what the children you looked after were like and what you're good at.

  6. Qualifications and sex-offence certificate

    If you have first aid or other relevant training, we verify it and show it on your profile. Every nanny also needs a Spanish sex-offence certificate, which Spanish law requires for anyone working with children.

  7. Your profile and introduction video

    We build your profile together: a natural photo, a short introduction video, your experience by age group, specialities, languages and skills. We check it over before it goes live.

  8. Approval

    Once your checks are done, you're approved for the bookings that match your verified experience. Some nannies start with certain types of booking while extra checks are finished.

  9. Matched bookings with a full brief

    We only send you bookings that suit your experience and preferences, along with the family's profile, the children's ages and needs, hours, location, responsibilities and what you can expect to earn.

  10. Accept or decline

    It's your call. If a booking isn't right for you, just say no. You'll never have to take work outside your comfort zone, your skills or your availability.

  11. Care, then get paid through us

    Once the booking's finished and any changes, like extra hours, are confirmed, we process your payment according to your agreed terms. Then you get to share feedback about the family too.

Clarity for both sides

When a booking becomes confirmed

An enquiry isn't a booking, and "we're interested" isn't a confirmation. A booking is only confirmed when all five of these are true.

This protects both sides. Families know their nanny is really reserved for them. Nannies aren't left keeping dates free for a booking nobody has paid for. The nanny's calendar is only officially blocked once all five are done.

Contact details follow confirmation. To protect everyone's privacy, phone numbers, email addresses and exact addresses are released only once a booking is confirmed.

  1. The booking details are agreed

    Dates, hours, location, children and responsibilities are written into the brief.

  2. The nanny has accepted

    Having seen the family profile and full brief.

  3. The family has accepted

    Having seen the nanny's profile, video and verification.

  4. Payment has been received

    The required payment or deposit is secured through us.

  5. The terms have been accepted

    Including the cancellation terms that apply to this booking.

One brief, shared by both sides

What the childcare brief includes

Each confirmed booking has one childcare brief, and the family and nanny both see the same version. Nobody should turn up and find the job is different from what they were told.

01

The people

  • Family and nanny profiles
  • Number of children and their ages
  • Personalities, interests and languages
  • Emergency contacts
02

The booking

  • Date, start time and expected finish
  • Location and type of childcare
  • Agreed responsibilities, and what's not included
  • Transport or driving, if any
03

The day-to-day

  • Routines, meals and bedtime
  • Activities and house rules
  • Swimming or pool requirements
  • Relevant allergies or medical information
04

The terms

  • Agreed price or rate
  • Travel expenses, where applicable
  • Additional-hours rate
  • Cancellation terms and booking conditions
05

Longer bookings

  • Working hours, breaks and days off
  • Accommodation, meals and sleeping arrangements
  • Flights and local transport
  • Itinerary changes and overnight responsibilities
06

Changing the brief

Big changes, like adding children, extending hours or adding driving, need the nanny's agreement before the booking starts, and may change the price.
