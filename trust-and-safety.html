Trust & Safety

Trust goes both ways.

Families need to know who is caring for their children. Nannies need to know who they're working for. Both need to know what happens if something unexpected happens. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what we do on each of those fronts.

What we check Booking policies

Our approach

Three questions every booking should answer

Most childcare services focus on one question. We built our process around three, because children get the best care when both adults around them have clear information and real support.

For families

"Who am I trusting with my children?"

Verified nanny profiles with clearly explained checks, introduction videos and a nanny matched to your children's real needs.

For nannies

"Who am I working for?"

Verified families, accurate information about the children and a written brief before any booking is accepted.

For everyone

"What if something goes wrong?"

Clear booking terms, an emergency procedure, factual incident reporting and a real person to contact.

Nanny verification

What we check, and what each badge means

Instead of one vague "verified" label, every nanny profile shows individual badges. Each badge means one specific thing has been checked, so you never have to guess.

Identity verified

We've checked the nanny's identity against appropriate official documents and, where relevant, their right to work. The documents themselves stay private and are never shown on a profile.

Video interview completed

We've spoken face to face on video about their experience, how they approach safety and their understanding of the role, and confirmed the person matches their application.

References checked

We've contacted referees directly, following our reference procedure, to ask about reliability, the children's ages, responsibilities and whether they'd hire the nanny again. Not just names on a form.

Experience verified

We've confirmed the childcare experience shown on the profile, including the age groups, through references and supporting information.

First aid verifiedQualification verified

We've seen evidence of a current certificate or qualification, such as first aid, CPR, early-years, newborn care or water-safety training. Certificates that expire need to be renewed to keep the badge.

Background check completed

Where legally appropriate and available for the nanny's country and situation, we've completed the stated background check, shown with the date it was completed. Available checks vary by country, so this badge is only shown when a check has actually been completed.

Profile reviewed

We've reviewed the nanny's photo, introduction video and written profile against our standards before publishing it.

Self-declared vs. verified skills

Some skills, like "confident swimmer", may be self-declared. Only formal qualifications we have actually seen, such as water-safety training or a lifeguard qualification, receive a verified badge. Profiles make the difference clear.

Verification reduces risk; it doesn't remove it. A badge means a clearly defined check has been completed, nothing more and nothing less. We keep verification current over time: references are refreshed, certificates are renewed and feedback from every booking informs our ongoing quality review.

Family verification

Nannies deserve the same confidence you do.

A nanny entering an unfamiliar home, or travelling abroad with a family, should know who they'll be working for. So families complete verification too, and nannies can see which steps are complete before they accept.

I've been on the nanny side of a booking where the information turned out to be wrong. Verifying families isn't about suspicion; it simply means everyone starts from accurate, agreed information, which makes for better care.

What families complete
  • Identity verified: the person booking is who they say they are.
  • Contact details verified: we can reach the parents before and during the booking.
  • Payment verified: payment is secured through us before confirmation.
  • Children's details completed: accurate ages, needs and relevant information.
  • Booking brief confirmed: hours, location, duties and expectations are agreed in writing.
  • Accommodation details: verified where appropriate, such as for longer or travel bookings.

Profile visibility & privacy

The right information, for the right people, at the right time

Profiles help families and nannies get to know each other, but personal information isn't automatically visible to everyone. We share information in layers.

  1. Public information

    Very limited, chosen by the person: a first name, approved photo, short introduction and selected skills. Children's photos are never made public by default.

  2. Matched profile

    When we propose a match, the family sees the nanny's full profile, video and badges, and the nanny sees the family profile, children's ages and interests and the booking brief.

  3. Confirmed booking information

    Phone numbers, email addresses, exact addresses and sensitive details like allergies are released only once a booking is confirmed, and only to the people who need them.

  4. Private records

    Identity documents, background-check records, full reference notes and bank details are kept privately by us and never appear on any profile.

Safety standards

What every nanny in our network agrees to

Every nanny understands these written standards before becoming active. They exist so that families know what to expect and nannies feel supported in holding professional boundaries.

Phone use

Phones are for communicating with parents, emergencies and booking logistics, not for scrolling while children need attention.

Photos of children

Children's privacy comes first. No photos without parents' permission, and never on social media or used for promotion without explicit authorisation.

Alcohol & substances

No alcohol or substances while working, including at weddings and events where adults may be drinking.

Water safety

Whether children can swim and whether swimming is expected is agreed in the brief. Families wanting significant swimming supervision are matched with nannies who have relevant, clearly labelled skills.

Transport & driving

Driving is never assumed. Where it's agreed, we expect a valid licence, appropriate insurance, the right child seats and the family's written consent.

Professional boundaries

No unauthorised visitors, confidentiality about the family, appropriate physical boundaries and calm, respectful guidance of children's behaviour.

Medication & allergies

Allergies are shared before the booking with clear instructions. Medication is only given following written parent instructions and within the nanny's competence.

Arrival safety check

Nannies are encouraged to do a quick check of unfamiliar hotels or villas: pools, balconies, stairs and doors. Families share any known risks in the brief.

Reporting concerns

Any nanny with a concern about a child's wellbeing knows how to report it, and any concern about their own safety too.

And what we ask of families

Trust and safety can't be the nanny's responsibility alone. Before a first booking, families agree to some simple, fair expectations.

  • Share accurate information, including relevant medical details and any illness
  • Provide emergency contacts and a safe environment
  • Return at the agreed time, or let us know early
  • Don't add children or change duties without agreement

If something happens

Our emergency procedure

Child safety comes first, always, and nothing commercial ever outweighs it. Every nanny is trained on a simple framework so they know exactly what to do, and in what order.

Immediate danger
  • Protect
  • Emergency services
  • Parents
  • Us
  • Document

Protect the child, contact emergency services where needed, then the parent or guardian, then us, and document what happened factually.

Non-urgent incident
  • Child safe
  • Parents
  • Us
  • Document

Make sure the child is safe and comfortable, let the parents know, contact us where appropriate and record what happened.

Before every booking, the nanny has the emergency information reasonably needed: parent and secondary contacts, the accommodation address, relevant medical and allergy information, and who is authorised to collect the children.

Incidents & disputes

Protect the child first. Treat the family fairly. Treat the nanny fairly. Review the facts.

When something goes wrong, we don't assume either side is right. We listen to both and look at what actually happened.

  1. Issue reported

    A family or nanny contacts us. Serious incidents are documented with factual information, not assumptions.

  2. Information reviewed

    We review the booking brief, messages and any incident report.

  3. Both sides heard

    We contact the family and the nanny separately and calmly.

  4. Resolution

    We resolve the matter according to the agreed terms and keep a confidential record. Serious concerns trigger an immediate internal review.

Data protection

Your information, handled with care

We're based in Spain and follow European data-protection law (GDPR). Childcare involves sensitive information, especially about children, so we treat it with particular care.

Read our privacy policy
  • Only what's needed. We collect only the information genuinely needed to provide safe childcare.
  • Children's information stays private. Details like a severe allergy are shared with your nanny for the booking, never shown on a public profile.
  • Health information is protected. Allergies, medication and medical details are seen only by the people who genuinely need them.
  • Documents stay with us. Identity and background-check records are kept securely and separately from profiles.
  • You stay in control. You can ask what we hold, why, who can see it, and exercise your data rights at any time.

Childcare involves real people and real life, so you won't see us claim that nothing can ever go wrong, or that a replacement will be available in every circumstance. Instead, we'd rather show you the systems behind every booking. Here's what we do promise.

Carefully verified

Clearly defined checks, each one explained.

Professionally matched

Clear protection

Written terms for both sides, shown before you pay.

Replacement assistance
