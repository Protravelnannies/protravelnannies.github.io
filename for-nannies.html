Why join us

Trust goes both ways, and that includes you.

Most childcare services only ask whether the family can trust the nanny. We also make sure you can trust the family. Here's what that means in practice.

01

Verified families

Families complete verification and a childcare brief before you're asked to accept, so you know who you'll be working for.

02

Accurate childcare briefs

Children's ages, routines, allergies, responsibilities and location, confirmed up front. No surprises at the door.

03

Clear hours and expectations

Start time, expected finish, duties and overtime terms are agreed before the booking begins, not negotiated on the night.

04

Payment through us

Bookings are paid through Pro Travel Nannies, with fair, transparent rates shown before you accept. No chasing families afterwards.

05

Respect for your reserved dates

When you block out days for a family, our cancellation terms recognise that commitment, so a late change of plans isn't simply your loss.

06

Real support

A sick child, a change of plans, a situation that doesn't feel right? There's a real person to contact, and clear policies to fall back on.

07

Choose your booking types

Tell us the ages, locations, hours and services you prefer: babysitting, holiday nanny, travel, events or overnight care.

08

International families

Work with international families from around the world, at hotels, villas, apartments and celebrations across Europe.

09

A professional profile and reputation

A profile and introduction video that show your real strengths and specialities, with verification families can see and feedback that builds over time.

Growing with you. Over time we plan to offer development opportunities for nannies in our network, starting with practical resources such as childcare-specific English for working with international families. Perfect English isn't automatically required if you're otherwise an excellent fit.

Who we're looking for

Quality over quantity.

We're not building the biggest nanny database. We're building a trusted network of childcare professionals that families are confident booking and nannies are proud to be part of.

Your job title matters less than your real experience, reliability, warmth and suitability for the kind of childcare you want to provide. You might be:

  • A professional nanny or experienced babysitter
  • An au pair with strong childcare experience
  • An early-years professional or teacher with childcare experience
  • A newborn or infant specialist
  • An experienced travel, wedding or event nanny
  • A multilingual childcare professional
  • A student with solid, referenced childcare experience
  • A retired or semi-retired childcare professional

We welcome applications from nannies across Europe. Our home network is in Spain, on the Costa del Sol, Málaga, the Balearic Islands and in Spain's major cities, and we're growing destination by destination.

The vetting process

A structured process, not an open directory

Nobody can simply create a profile and start accepting bookings. Every nanny goes through the same careful steps.

Why it protects you too

A careful process means families trust the network, and that trust is what lets them book confidently, pay properly and treat you as the professional you are.

It also means your verified skills are shown accurately on your profile, so you're matched with bookings that genuinely suit you.

  1. Apply online

    Tell us about your experience, the ages you've cared for, languages, qualifications, specialities, availability and two referees. It takes around 15 minutes.

  2. Initial review

    We personally read every application, looking at experience, communication and fit with our standards.

  3. Video interview

    A friendly, relaxed conversation about your experience, how you approach safety and the kind of bookings you'd love.

  4. Identity and documents

    We verify your identity and, where relevant, your right to work and your qualifications.

  5. References

    We contact your referees directly, rather than just collecting names on a form.

  6. Background check, where available

    Where legally appropriate and available for your situation, we complete a relevant background check, and say exactly what was checked.

  7. Your profile goes live

    With your photo, introduction video and the verification badges you've earned. Then we start matching you with families.

What you'll need

Have these ready

You don't need everything to start your application. We'll ask for documents at the right stage, and only collect what's genuinely needed.

Required
  • A valid government-issued photo ID or passport
  • Proof of your right to work in the country where you'll work, where appropriate
  • Two childcare references we can contact directly
  • A clear, friendly profile photo
  • A short introduction video (we'll guide you)
Where you have them
  • First aid or paediatric first aid and CPR certificates
  • Childcare or early-years qualifications
  • Newborn-care or safeguarding training
  • Swimming, water-safety or lifeguard qualifications
  • A driving licence, if you're happy to drive
  • A criminal-record or background certificate, where available

Our nanny standards

What families can expect from every nanny

Every nanny agrees to our written network standards before becoming active. They protect the children, the families and you.

Professional behaviour

Warm, respectful and reliable, with appropriate boundaries with children and families.

Child safety first

Children's safety comes before everything, including constant, attentive supervision.

Communication & punctuality

Arriving on time, keeping families informed and responding promptly to messages.

Confidentiality

Families' privacy, homes, routines and personal details are always kept confidential.

Phones & social media

Phones used only when needed while working. No photos of children without the parents' consent, and never on personal social media.

No alcohol or substances

No alcohol or substances while working, including at weddings and events.

Transport

Driving or transporting children only when agreed in the brief, with appropriate car seats.

Swimming & water safety

Pool and beach time only as agreed, always with close, undistracted supervision.

Emergencies & concerns

Knowing what to do in an emergency, and reporting any incident or concern to the family and to us promptly.

Questions

Nanny FAQs

How much will I earn?

Rates depend on the type of booking, the number of children, hours and location. You'll always see fair, transparent rates, including any travel or overtime terms, before you accept a booking. We don't build our business by underpaying the people who care for children.

Do I have to accept every booking I'm offered?

No. You tell us the ages, locations, hours and services you prefer, and you see the full childcare brief before deciding whether to accept.

What happens if a family cancels?

Every booking has clear cancellation terms that both sides see before confirming. They're designed to be fair to families and to recognise the dates you've reserved, especially for multi-day holiday bookings.

Do I need perfect English?

Not necessarily. Most families communicate in English, so you'll need to be clear and confident, and your profile shows your English level honestly. Other languages are a real bonus: many families ask for a nanny who speaks their own language.

Can I join if I'm a student or work part-time?

Yes, as long as you have solid, referenced childcare experience and can be reliable for the bookings you accept. Many bookings are evenings, weekends and holidays.

How long does the process take?

We aim to review applications within a few working days. After that, the time depends mainly on scheduling your video interview and hearing back from your referees.
