Why join us

Trust goes both ways, and that includes you.

Most agencies only ask whether a family can trust the nanny. We think you should be able to trust the family too. Here's how that works day to day.

01

Verified families

Families are verified and fill in a childcare brief before we ask you to accept, so you know who you're going to.

02

Accurate childcare briefs

Ages, routines, allergies, what you'll be doing and where, all confirmed before you go. You won't turn up expecting a baby and find a ten-year-old.

03

Clear hours and expectations

Start time, expected finish, duties and overtime are agreed before the booking starts. Nobody's haggling with you at 11pm.

04

Payment through us

Families pay through Pro Travel Nannies, and you see the rate before you accept. You won't be chasing anyone for money afterwards.

05

Respect for your reserved dates

If you block out days for a family, our cancellation terms take that seriously. A last-minute change of plans shouldn't land entirely on you.

06

Real support

Child woken up with a fever? Plans changed? Something feels off? You can reach a real person, and there are clear policies to lean on.

07

Choose your booking types

Let us know the ages, places, hours and services you like best, from babysitting and holiday nannying to travel, events or overnight care.

08

International families

Meet families from all over the world, in hotels, villas, apartments and at celebrations around Europe.

09

A professional profile and reputation

Your profile and intro video show what you're actually good at. Families can see your verification, and your feedback grows with each booking.

Growing with you. Over time we'd like to offer development opportunities for nannies in the network, starting with practical things like childcare English for working with international families. And if your English isn't perfect but you're otherwise a great fit, that doesn't rule you out.

Who we're looking for

We'd rather be small and good.

I'm not trying to build the biggest nanny database in Europe. I want a network of people families feel good about booking, and that nannies are happy to say they belong to.

Your job title matters less than your experience, whether you turn up when you say you will, and how you are with children. You might be:

  • A professional nanny or experienced babysitter
  • An au pair with strong childcare experience
  • An early-years professional or teacher with childcare experience
  • A newborn or infant specialist
  • An experienced travel, wedding or event nanny
  • A multilingual childcare professional
  • A student with solid, referenced childcare experience
  • A retired or semi-retired childcare professional

We'd love to hear from nannies anywhere in Europe. Our home base is Spain, on the Costa del Sol, in Málaga, the Balearic Islands and the bigger cities, and we're adding new places as we grow.

The vetting process

You can't just sign up and start

Nobody makes a profile and starts taking bookings the same day. Every nanny goes through the same steps, no shortcuts.

Why it protects you too

When families trust the network, they book with confidence, pay properly and treat you like the professional you are. That trust comes from the checks.

It also means your verified skills show up properly on your profile, so we can match you with bookings that actually suit you.

  1. Apply online

    Tell us about your experience, the ages you've looked after, your languages, qualifications, specialities and availability, plus two referees. It takes around 15 minutes.

  2. Initial review

    A real person reads your application. We look at your experience, how you communicate and whether you'd fit our standards.

  3. Video interview

    A relaxed chat about your experience, how you think about safety and the kind of bookings you'd love to do. It's not an interrogation.

  4. Identity and documents

    We check your ID and, where it applies, your right to work and your qualifications.

  5. References

    We actually get in touch with your referees. We don't just collect names on a form.

  6. Sex-offence certificate

    In Spain, anyone who works with children has to hold a sex-offence certificate by law. We check the original before you take a booking. If you're not a Spanish national, we'll also need the equivalent from your country of nationality.

  7. Your profile goes live

    Your photo, intro video and the verification badges you've earned go up, and then we start matching you with families.

What you'll need

Have these ready

You don't need all of this to start. We'll ask for documents when we get to that stage, and we only collect what we need.

Required
  • A valid government-issued photo ID or passport
  • Proof of your right to work in the country where you'll work, where appropriate
  • A Spanish sex-offence certificate (Certificado de Delitos de Naturaleza Sexual), plus the equivalent from your home country if you're not Spanish. It's free, and we'll help you request it
  • Two childcare references we can contact directly
  • A clear, friendly profile photo
  • A short introduction video (we'll guide you)
Where you have them
  • First aid or paediatric first aid and CPR certificates
  • Childcare or early-years qualifications
  • Newborn-care or safeguarding training
  • Swimming, water-safety or lifeguard qualifications
  • A driving licence, if you're happy to drive

Our nanny standards

What families can expect from our nannies

Before anyone goes live, they sign up to our written standards. They're there to protect the children and the families, and you as well.

Professional behaviour

Kind and reliable, with sensible boundaries with the children and the family.

Child safety first

The children's safety comes first, always. That means keeping a proper eye on them the whole time.

Communication & punctuality

Turning up on time, keeping parents in the loop and answering messages quickly.

Confidentiality

What you see and hear in a family's home stays private. That includes their routines and personal details.

Phones & social media

Phones away unless you need them. No photos of the children without the parents' OK, and never on your own social media.

No alcohol or substances

No alcohol or substances while working, and yes, that includes weddings and events.

Transport

Only driving or transporting children if it's agreed in the brief, and with the right car seats.

Swimming & water safety

Pool and beach time only as agreed, and you stay close and watching the whole time.

Emergencies & concerns

Knowing what to do if something goes wrong, and telling the family and us straight away about any incident or worry.

Questions

Nanny FAQs

How much will I earn?

It depends on the type of booking, how many children, the hours and where it is. You'll always see the rate, plus any travel or overtime terms, before you accept. We're not going to build this business by underpaying the people looking after children.

Do I have to accept every booking I'm offered?

No. You tell us what ages, places, hours and services you prefer, and you see the full childcare brief before you decide.

What happens if a family cancels?

Each booking has clear cancellation terms that you and the family both see before confirming. They're meant to be fair to families while respecting the dates you've kept free, especially on multi-day holiday bookings.

Do I need perfect English?

Not necessarily. Most families speak to us in English, so you'll need to be clear and confident, and your profile will show your level honestly. Other languages are a big plus, because lots of families ask for a nanny who speaks theirs.

Can I join if I'm a student or work part-time?

Yes, as long as you've got solid childcare experience with references and you can be relied on for the bookings you take. Plenty of bookings fall on evenings, weekends and holidays anyway.

How long does the process take?

We try to look at applications within a few working days. After that it mostly comes down to finding a time for your video interview and hearing back from your referees.
