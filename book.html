Your childcare brief

Tell us about your family

The more you tell us, the better the match. It takes about five minutes, and you won't pay anything until you've seen your nanny's profile and decided to go ahead.

What happens next

  1. We read your brief ourselves: no bots, a real person goes through it.
  2. We match you with checked nannies who suit your children's ages, languages and plans.
  3. You see their profiles, which checks they've passed and their intro videos.
  4. You confirm once you've seen the price and booking terms.

You don't pay anything until you confirm.

Why so many questions? Your nanny reads this same brief, so they'll turn up already knowing your children's ages, routines and needs. The better the information, the better the care.
