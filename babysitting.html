How booking works

Simple to book, carefully matched

You tell us what you need. We do the thinking about who's right for your family.

Booking ahead gives us the best chance of matching you with the ideal nanny, especially in summer and around public holidays. If you need someone at short notice, ask anyway. We'll always tell you honestly what's possible.

Start your request

  1. Share your plans

    Where you're staying, the date and times, your children's ages and anything we should know.

  2. Meet your match

    We personally review your request and introduce a verified nanny with a profile and introduction video.

  3. Confirm and pay securely

    Your times, price and terms are confirmed in writing. Your nanny's evening is then reserved for you.

  4. Enjoy your time

    Your nanny arrives a little early for a handover. Afterwards, rebooking the same nanny is easy.

Why a 3-hour minimum?

Every booking involves matching, preparation and travel for your nanny. A short minimum keeps bookings fair for the nannies who care for your children, and gives your evening room to breathe.

An example

Two children, 19:00 to 23:00 while you have dinner: 4 hours × €36 = €144 for childcare. Anything else, such as late-night transport, is shown clearly before you pay.

Good to know

Possible extras, always shown before payment

Most bookings are simply the hourly rate. In some situations an additional charge may apply. If it does, you'll see it clearly before you pay, never afterwards.

Overtime

If you're back later than planned, the extra time is recorded and charged at the agreed rate, and your nanny is paid for it. Running late? Just let your nanny know.

Late-night transport

If a booking ends late or your accommodation is remote, a taxi may be needed so your nanny gets home safely. We'll agree this in advance.

Last-minute requests

Very short-notice bookings take extra work to arrange well, so a last-minute fee may apply. We'll always tell you first.

Public holidays & peak dates

Dates such as Christmas, New Year's Eve and Easter may carry a premium rate, which also goes towards your nanny's pay.

Questions

Babysitting FAQs

What ages do your babysitters care for?

We match each family with a nanny whose verified experience fits your children's ages. Tell us about your children in your request, including babies and toddlers, and we'll find someone suited to them.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for summer weekends, holidays and popular destinations. Booking ahead gives us more choice when matching. If your plans are last-minute, still get in touch and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Can my nanny put the children to bed?

Yes. Evening bookings often include dinner, bath, stories and bedtime. Share your usual routine in your booking brief and your nanny will follow it as closely as possible.

Can my babysitter supervise swimming?

Only if it's agreed in advance. Please tell us whether your accommodation has a pool, whether your children swim and whether you'd like them to swim during the booking. We'll match you with a nanny with suitable swimming experience wherever possible, and your nanny's profile shows which water-related skills are verified and which are self-declared.

Will the nanny care for children from another family too?

If you're travelling with friends and would like one nanny to care for both families' children, tell us. Staffing is always decided by safety, so depending on the number and ages of the children we may recommend a second nanny.

What if I'm running late?

Send your nanny a message as soon as you know. Additional time is charged at the agreed rate so your nanny is paid fairly for it.

Can I book the same babysitter again?

Of course. If your family loved your nanny, you can request them again for another evening, or for longer as a holiday nanny.
