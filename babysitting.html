Who it's for

Everyone needs a bit of time off on holiday, parents included

Wanting a few hours to yourselves doesn't make you bad parents. Honestly, it often makes the whole trip happier. These are the times families usually get in touch.

01

Date nights & dinners

A long dinner by the sea, or that restaurant you've had your eye on since you booked the trip, while the kids settle in for the night.

02

Spa time & shopping

A massage, a round of golf, or a slow wander round the old town without anyone asking for ice cream.

03

Excursions & activities

The boat trip, wine tasting or day out that really isn't made for little legs.

04

Pool & beach time

An extra pair of eyes and hands by the pool for the afternoon, so you get to relax as well.

05

Bedtime support

Bath, stories and lights out in a strange room. Your nanny keeps it calm and sticks to your home routine as closely as they can.

06

Working or resting

A few quiet hours to take a call, clear your inbox or have a proper nap. Local families book us for this too.

How booking works

Easy for you. We do the matching.

You tell us what you need, and we work out who'd be the right fit for your family.

The earlier you book, the better our chances of finding the right person, especially in summer and around public holidays. Need someone at short notice? Ask anyway. We'll tell you straight whether we can make it work.

Start your request

  1. Share your plans

    Where you're staying, the date and times, your children's ages and anything we should know.

  2. Meet your match

    We read through your request ourselves and introduce you to a verified nanny, with their profile and introduction video.

  3. Confirm and pay securely

    You get your times, price and terms in writing, and your nanny keeps the evening free for you.

  4. Enjoy your time

    Your nanny comes a little early so you can do a proper handover. If the kids loved them, booking them again is easy.

Why a 3-hour minimum?

Behind each booking there's matching, prep and your nanny's journey to you. A short minimum keeps it fair for the nannies, and it means you're not rushing back halfway through dessert.

An example

Say you've got two children and you're out for dinner from 19:00 to 23:00. That's 4 hours × €36 = €144 for childcare. If anything else applies, like late-night transport, you'll see it before you pay.

Good to know

The occasional extra, and you'll always see it first

Most of the time you just pay the hourly rate. Now and then an extra charge might apply, and if it does, you'll see it before you pay. Never afterwards.

Overtime

Back later than planned? It happens. The extra time is recorded and charged at the agreed rate, and your nanny gets paid for it. Just send them a message.

Late-night transport

If it's a late finish or you're staying somewhere remote, your nanny might need a taxi to get home safely. We'll agree this with you beforehand.

Last-minute requests

Finding the right person at very short notice takes a lot of extra work, so a last-minute fee may apply. We'll always tell you first.

Public holidays & peak dates

Dates like Christmas, New Year's Eve and Easter may have a premium rate. That extra goes towards your nanny's pay as well.

Questions

Babysitting FAQs

What ages do your babysitters care for?

We match you with a nanny whose verified experience fits your children's ages. Tell us about your kids in your request, babies and toddlers included, and we'll find someone who's right for them.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for summer weekends, holidays and the busier destinations. The more notice we have, the more nannies we can choose from. Last-minute plans? Get in touch anyway and we'll be honest about what's possible.

Can my nanny put the children to bed?

Yes, and lots of evening bookings include dinner, bath, stories and bed. Pop your usual routine in the booking brief and your nanny will stick to it as closely as they can.

Can my babysitter supervise swimming?

Only if it's agreed beforehand. Let us know if there's a pool where you're staying, whether your children can swim, and whether you'd like them in the water during the booking. Wherever we can, we'll match you with a nanny who has the right swimming experience. Their profile shows which water skills we've verified and which they've declared themselves.

Will the nanny care for children from another family too?

If you're away with friends and want one nanny for both families' kids, just tell us. We always decide staffing on safety, so depending on how many children there are and how old they are, we might suggest a second nanny.

What if I'm running late?

Message your nanny as soon as you know. Extra time is charged at the agreed rate, so they're paid fairly for staying on.

Can I book the same babysitter again?

Of course. If the kids loved them, ask for the same nanny again for another evening, or for longer as a holiday nanny.
