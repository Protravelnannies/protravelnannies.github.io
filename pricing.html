Possible extras

Extras, agreed before you book

Most bookings don't have any. If one does apply, we'll explain it and it'll be in your total before you pay.

01

Additional hours

If you'd like your nanny to stay later, ask as early as you can. They'll stay if they're able to. We record the extra time and charge it at the agreed rate, and your nanny gets paid for it.

02

Travel & transport

If your nanny has to come a long way, there may be costs like mileage, parking, a train, a ferry or an airport transfer. We agree these with you before confirming.

03

Late-night taxi

If a booking finishes late, we may add a taxi so your nanny gets home safely.

04

Last-minute bookings

Finding the right nanny within 12–24 hours takes a lot more running around. A last-minute fee may apply, and you'll see it before you pay.

05

Public holidays & peak dates

Higher rates may apply over Christmas, New Year's Eve, Easter, public holidays and big local events. The nannies working those dates get paid more too.

06

Changes to a booking

Bigger changes, like adding a child, longer hours or asking your nanny to drive, need their OK first and may change the price.

Cancellations

Fair for families, fair for nannies

Once a booking's confirmed, your nanny keeps that time for you and may say no to other work. Our cancellation terms take that into account, without being harsh.

  1. Well in advance

    If you cancel well ahead of time, it's free or costs very little.

  2. Closer to the date

    The charge goes up as the booking gets closer.

  3. Same day or very late

    The charge is higher again, because your nanny has kept the time free for you.

Holiday and travel nanny bookings have their own terms, since the nanny is holding several days or weeks. You'll see the exact terms for your booking before you pay.

Pricing questions

What parents ask about cost

Is the hourly rate per child or per booking?

Per booking. One rate covers all the children: €32/hour for one child, €36 for two, €40 for three and €44 for four.

Why is there a 3-hour minimum?

Nannies often travel quite a way to get to hotels and villas, and we do the same matching and prep whether it's two hours or ten. The 3-hour minimum keeps short bookings fair for the nanny and means we don't cut corners on the small ones.

Are these prices fixed?

They're our launch prices, so we'll review them over time. But your price is confirmed in writing before you pay, and it won't change unless you agree to something new, like extra hours.

What if we come back later than planned?

Message your nanny as soon as you know. If they can stay, we record the extra time and charge it at the agreed additional-hours rate, and your nanny gets paid for it. Just don't assume they're free past the agreed finish time.

Do you charge more for more than four children?

With bigger groups, or when the children's ages, a pool or the setting mean one nanny isn't enough, we may suggest a second nanny. Staffing always comes down to safety. We'll explain the cost in your quote.

Do I need to pay my nanny's travel costs?

Only if there are any, for example a long journey, a late-night taxi, or flights and accommodation for a travel nanny. We agree them with you and show them before you confirm.

Do I pay the nanny directly?

No, you pay securely through us and we pay your nanny on their agreed terms. It's easier for you, and it protects both of you.

Is there a service fee on top?

If your booking has a service fee, it shows up as its own line in your booking summary, next to the childcare cost and any travel costs. So you'll know the total before you pay.
