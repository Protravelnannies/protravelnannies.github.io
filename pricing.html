Possible extras

Extras that may apply, always agreed up front

Most bookings have no extras at all. When something does apply, it's explained and shown in your total before you pay.

01

Additional hours

If you'd like your nanny to stay later, ask as early as you can. Your nanny agrees where reasonably possible, the extra time is recorded, and it's charged at the agreed rate, with your nanny paid for it too.

02

Travel & transport

Where a nanny travels a significant distance, costs like mileage, parking, train, ferry or airport transfers may apply. These are agreed before confirmation.

03

Late-night taxi

When a booking ends late, a taxi home for your nanny may be added so they can travel back safely.

04

Last-minute bookings

Childcare needed within 12–24 hours takes extra work to arrange well. A last-minute fee may apply, and is always shown before you pay.

05

Public holidays & peak dates

Premium rates may apply on dates such as Christmas, New Year's Eve, Easter, public holidays and major local events. Nannies working these dates benefit too.

06

Changes to a booking

Significant changes, like adding children, extending hours or adding driving, need the nanny's agreement and may change the price.

Cancellations

Fair for families, fair for nannies

Once a booking is confirmed, your nanny reserves that time for you and may turn down other work. Our cancellation terms reflect that, gently.

  1. Well in advance

    Cancelling well ahead of the booking is free or low-cost.

  2. Closer to the date

    The cancellation charge increases as the booking approaches.

  3. Same day or very late

    Stronger protection applies, because the nanny has kept the time free for you.

Holiday and travel nanny bookings have their own terms because nannies reserve several days or weeks. The exact terms for your booking are always shown before you pay.

Pricing questions

Good questions about cost

Is the hourly rate per child or per booking?

Per booking. The rate covers all the children in the booking: €32/hour for one child, €36 for two, €40 for three and €44 for four.

Why is there a 3-hour minimum?

Nannies often travel to reach hotels and villas, and every booking involves matching and preparation. A 3-hour minimum keeps shorter bookings fair for the nanny and lets us give every booking the same care.

Are these prices fixed?

These are our launch prices. We'll review them over time, but the price for your booking is always confirmed in writing before you pay and won't change unless you agree to a change, such as extra hours.

What if we come back later than planned?

Let your nanny know as early as you can. If they're able to stay, the extra time is recorded and charged at the agreed additional-hours rate, and your nanny is paid for it. Please don't assume a nanny is available beyond the agreed finish time.

Do you charge more for more than four children?

For larger groups, or where children's ages, swimming or the setting mean one nanny wouldn't be right, we may recommend a second nanny. Safety always decides staffing. We'll quote this clearly.

Do I need to pay my nanny's travel costs?

Only where they apply, for example for long distances, late-night taxis or travel nanny flights and accommodation. Any travel costs are agreed and shown before you confirm.

Do I pay the nanny directly?

No. Payment is made securely through us, and we pay your nanny according to their agreed terms. This keeps things simple for you and protects both sides.

Is there a service fee on top?

If a service fee applies to your booking, it appears as its own line in your booking summary, alongside the childcare cost and any travel costs, so you can see the total before you pay.
