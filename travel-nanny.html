On the move

When you'll be glad of another pair of hands

If you've ever travelled with small children, you'll know getting there is often the hardest bit. A travel nanny gives you some breathing room, and gives your children the same person to lean on throughout.

  • Airports: check-in, security and boarding with children and bags
  • Flights: snacks, play, naps and comfort in the air
  • Transfers: car journeys, ferries and arrivals at your accommodation
  • Hotel and villa stays: daily childcare wherever you're staying
  • Routines: meals, naps and bedtime kept steady between destinations
  • Activities: days out and excursions where another adult makes life easier

Your nanny shouldn't end up out of pocket for travelling with you, and you shouldn't get any nasty surprises. So your quote lists everything before you confirm.

Covered by the family

Travel and living costs

  • Flights, trains or ferries for the nanny
  • Accommodation, including a private room where applicable

No surprises. Each cost is in your quote and agreed in writing before we confirm your booking.

Good care needs rest

Rest and days off, sorted before you go

Travelling with a family doesn't mean being on call 24 hours a day. A nanny who's had proper rest takes better care of your children, so rest is built into each travel booking.

I've been the nanny on long trips where nobody talked about hours or days off beforehand, and it gets awkward for everyone. Sorting it out at the start looks after the family and the nanny, and your children get better care because of it.

Cameron

Your agreement will set out

  • Daily working hours and how travel days are counted
  • Daily breaks and rest between working days
  • Days off during longer trips
  • Any evening or overnight responsibilities
  • How additional hours are agreed and paid
  • Sleeping arrangements and private space
  • What's included in the role, and what isn't

Questions

Travel nanny FAQs

Can the nanny join us from outside Spain?

Lots of travel nanny trips start abroad, with a flight from London, say. Tell us where you're starting from and we'll talk through what's possible.

How far in advance should I ask?

As early as you can. Travel bookings take more planning, and the right nanny needs time to clear their calendar. A few weeks' notice gives us the best chance of finding someone great.

Does the nanny need their own room?

For trips over several days, your nanny will need a suitable private place to sleep. We'll agree the details with you before confirming.

Will the nanny care for the children at night?

Only if that's agreed beforehand. Any overnight duties are written into your agreement separately and priced on their own. Have a look at overnight care.

What if our plans change mid-trip?

Tell us as soon as you can. Your agreement explains how changes to the itinerary work, and we'll help everyone adjust in a way that's fair.

How is the price calculated?

Each travel nanny booking gets its own quote. That's the professional nanny fee plus the agreed travel costs, like flights, accommodation, transport and meals. You'll see the full total before you confirm.
