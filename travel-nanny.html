On the move

Where an extra pair of hands matters most

Anyone who has travelled with small children knows the journey is often the hardest part. A travel nanny gives you flexibility, and gives your children consistency.

  • Airports: check-in, security and boarding with children and bags
  • Flights: snacks, play, naps and comfort in the air
  • Transfers: car journeys, ferries and arrivals at your accommodation
  • Hotel and villa stays: daily childcare wherever you're staying
  • Routines: meals, naps and bedtime kept steady between destinations
  • Activities: days out and excursions where an extra adult helps

Good care needs rest

Rest periods and days off, agreed up front

Travelling with a family is not the same as being on call 24 hours a day. A well-rested nanny is a better nanny, so rest is built into every travel booking.

I've been the nanny on long trips where nobody had talked about hours or days off beforehand. It's uncomfortable for everyone. Agreeing it at the start protects the family and the nanny, and it means better care for your children.

Cameron

Your agreement will set out

  • Daily working hours and how travel days are counted
  • Daily breaks and rest between working days
  • Days off during longer trips
  • Any evening or overnight responsibilities
  • How additional hours are agreed and paid
  • Sleeping arrangements and private space
  • What's included in the role, and what isn't

Questions

Travel nanny FAQs

Can the nanny join us from outside Spain?

Many travel nanny journeys begin abroad, for example a flight from London. Tell us where your trip starts and we'll discuss what's possible for your itinerary.

How far in advance should I ask?

As early as possible. Travel bookings need more planning, and the right nanny needs time to reserve their calendar. A few weeks' notice gives us the best chance of a great match.

Does the nanny need their own room?

For multi-day travel, your nanny will need appropriate private sleeping space. We'll agree the exact arrangements with you before confirmation.

Will the nanny care for the children at night?

Only if it's agreed in advance. Overnight responsibilities are set out separately in your agreement and priced accordingly. See overnight care.

What if our plans change mid-trip?

Let us know as soon as you can. Your agreement explains how itinerary changes are handled, and we'll help everyone adjust fairly.

How is the price calculated?

Every travel nanny booking receives a custom quote: the professional nanny fee plus agreed travel costs such as flights, accommodation, transport and meals. You'll see the complete total before you confirm.
