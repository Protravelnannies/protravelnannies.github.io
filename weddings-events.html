Four ways we can help

Pick what suits your event

No two events are alike, so you can use any of these four on their own or mix them.

01

Individual wedding babysitting

A family books a nanny just for their own children during the event, either at the venue or back at the hotel or villa, while the parents get to enjoy themselves.

Ideal for: guests travelling with young children.

02

Children's room

A room set aside for the children at or near the venue, with games, quiet corners, food and somewhere calm for the little ones to sleep. We staff it with the right number of nannies.

Ideal for: weddings with several families and a range of ages.

03

Wedding childcare team

For bigger events with lots of children: several verified nannies, with a lead nanny running the team. We plan it around your timeline, from the ceremony right through to the late-night dancing.

Ideal for: large celebrations and multi-day weddings.

04

Dedicated wedding nanny

One nanny sticks with one family for the whole wedding or event. Getting ready, the ceremony, photos, the reception, and then bedtime.

Ideal for: the couple's own children, or close family.

Staffing decided by safety

How we decide how many nannies you need

Weddings mean mixed ages, venues the kids don't know, pools, crowds and late nights. So we work out staffing from the children who are actually coming, rather than a set formula.

Before we confirm an event, we look at:

  • The number of children and their ages, including babies
  • The venue, its layout and any pools or water nearby
  • The length of the event and how late it runs
  • Planned activities, meals and sleeping arrangements
  • Whether a lead nanny should coordinate the team

If we think you need more nannies than you asked for, we'll be honest and explain why.

Planning timeline

From your first message to the last dance

Childcare goes best when it's planned along with everything else for the day. This is how it usually works.

The sooner you get in touch, the more nannies we have to choose from, especially in the middle of wedding season.

Start planning

  1. As early as possible

    Tell us your date, the venue, roughly how many children and what kind of setup you're thinking of.

  2. Custom proposal

    We suggest a format and how many nannies, and send you a clear custom quote with any travel costs included.

  3. Confirm and reserve

    Once you're happy, your nannies' dates are reserved and held for you.

  4. Children's details

    Nearer the time, we collect each child's age, allergies, routines and their parents' contact details.

  5. Final briefing

    Before the event, your nannies get the timeline, venue details and emergency plan.

  6. The celebration

    Your nannies get there in good time, help the children settle in and keep things calm right up to the last goodnight.

Questions

Wedding & event FAQs

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as you've got your date and a rough idea of how many children are coming. Bigger teams take longer to put together, and wedding season gets busy fast.

Where will the children be cared for?

It depends on the event. It could be a children's room at the venue, a separate space nearby, or each family's own accommodation. We'll suggest what works best for the ages coming.

Can the nannies put children to bed during the reception?

Yes. A lot of parents like having a quiet place for the little ones to sleep. We'll sort it out with the venue and the parents beforehand.

Do you need details about every child?

Yes. For each child we look after, we ask for their age, any allergies or medical information, and a parent's contact details. The more we know, the better we can look after them.

Can guests book individually?

Of course. Guests can book their own nanny, or couples and planners can arrange childcare for everyone. We can also send you details to pass on to your guests.
