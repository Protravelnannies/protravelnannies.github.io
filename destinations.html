Where we care

Based in Spain. Caring across Europe.

We live in Spain, and our nannies are on the Costa del Sol, in Málaga, on the Balearic Islands and in Spain's big cities. From here we look after international families travelling all over Europe, and we work the same way wherever you are.

Andalucía

Marbella & the Costa del Sol

Beach clubs, golf and family villas in the sun. International families love this stretch of coast, and plenty of them come back every summer.

Whether you're in a hotel on the beach or a villa up in the hills with its own pool, we can find you a nanny who knows the area, so you can have that long dinner, go to the party or enjoy a day out.

Andalucía

Málaga

A busy, fun city with museums, beaches and an old centre. For a lot of families it's where a holiday in southern Spain starts.

  • City stays: babysitting at your hotel or apartment while you wander the old town or go out for dinner
  • Airport arrivals: a travel nanny who meets you when you land and helps with the transfer
  • Coast and countryside: holiday nannies if you're staying in a villa outside the city

Pools and the sea

Island holidays usually mean water somewhere. Tell us about the pool, beach or boat in your booking brief, and we'll try to match you with a nanny who has the right swimming experience. Profiles show which water skills we've checked and which the nanny has told us about.

Yachts

Looking after children on a yacht isn't like babysitting at a villa. You have to think about water safety, the sea, where everyone sleeps and what happens in an emergency. So we handle yacht bookings as specialist requests. Get in touch to talk it through.
