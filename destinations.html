Andalucía

Marbella & the Costa del Sol

Sunshine, beach clubs, golf and family villas. It's one of Spain's best-loved destinations for international families, and many come back summer after summer.

From beachfront hotels to hillside villas with private pools, we help families enjoy long dinners, celebrations and days out, with a trusted nanny who knows the area.

Málaga

A lively city with museums, beaches and a historic centre, and for many families the first stop on a holiday in southern Spain.

Pools and the sea

Many island stays include pools, beaches and boat days. Tell us about water access in your booking brief, and we'll match you with a nanny with suitable swimming experience wherever possible. Profiles show which water skills are verified and which are self-declared.

Yachts

Childcare on board a yacht is different from babysitting at a villa: water safety, sea conditions, sleeping arrangements and emergency plans all matter. We treat yacht childcare as a specialist request. Get in touch to talk it through.
