This policy is provided in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (Ley Orgánica 3/2018, "LOPDGDD"). It applies to families, nannies and anyone who visits our website or contacts us.

Summary

  • We collect only what we genuinely need to match families with nannies and to provide safe childcare.
  • Information about children's health (such as allergies or medication) is collected only with a parent's or guardian's explicit consent, and shared only with the nanny caring for that child and the staff who need it.
  • Before a booking is confirmed, families and nannies see only limited profile details. Phone numbers, email addresses and exact addresses are shared only once a booking is confirmed.
  • Identity documents, background-check results, full references and bank details are kept in private records and are never shown on profiles.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can access, correct or delete your data, and complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

When you send a childcare request, create a family profile or make a booking, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: names of the parents/guardians, email, phone/WhatsApp number, country of residence, preferred language.
  • Verification information: confirmation of identity (for example, checking an ID document) and of contact and payment details, so that nannies know who they will be working for. [Confirm exactly which checks are performed and whether copies of ID documents are kept or only viewed.]
  • Booking information: destination, dates and times, accommodation (hotel, villa, apartment or yacht) and its address, type of service, duties agreed, travel arrangements and itinerary for travel nanny bookings, and the agreed price.
  • Children's information: see section 3.
  • Emergency contacts: name and phone number of a person to call in an emergency. Please tell them you have shared their details with us.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment provider, [payment provider]. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details (such as the last four digits of a card), but not your full card number.
  • Optional family introduction: a short family description and, if you choose, a family photo or introduction video.
  • Communications and feedback: messages with us, feedback and reviews after bookings, complaints and any incident reports.

3. Children's information

We never collect information directly from children. Information about children is provided by their parent or legal guardian, and we follow one simple principle: we collect only what is genuinely necessary to provide safe childcare.

Depending on the booking, this may include:

  • first name, age and number of children;
  • routines (meals, naps, bedtime), languages spoken, interests and activities;
  • behavioural or developmental information you consider important for their care;
  • swimming ability and any rules about pools, beaches or water;
  • health information, such as allergies, medical conditions, medication, disabilities, health-related dietary needs and emergency medical information.

Health information is "special category" data under article 9 of the GDPR. We process it only with the explicit consent of the child's parent or legal guardian (article 9(2)(a) GDPR), given separately for this purpose. In an emergency where a child's life or health is at risk and consent cannot be obtained, we or the nanny may share it with medical or emergency services to protect the child's vital interests (article 9(2)(c) GDPR).

Children's health information:

  • is shared only with the nanny assigned to the confirmed booking and with the staff who need it to arrange or support that booking;
  • is never displayed on a general family profile or in marketing; for example, a nanny may need to know "Child has a severe nut allergy", but this would never appear publicly;
  • is kept separately from general customer and enquiry records, with restricted access; and
  • is deleted or anonymised in line with section 10.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. However, if a nanny does not have the health information needed to care for a child safely, we may be unable to confirm or continue the booking.

Photos and videos of children are never published on our website, social media or marketing without the specific written permission of a parent or legal guardian. Nannies are not permitted to take, keep or share photos or videos of children except as agreed with the family (see our Terms).

4. Information we collect from nannies

When you apply to join our network or work through us, we may collect:

  • Application details: name, contact details, location, date of birth, languages and English level, childcare experience by age group, CV and employment history, availability, skills (for example, swimming or driving) and your answers in our application and video interview.
  • Identity and right-to-work documents: passport, national ID card, NIE/TIE or residence/work authorisation, to verify your identity and that you are legally permitted to work in Spain.
  • References: referees' names and contact details, and what they tell us. Please tell your referees you have shared their details with us.
  • Qualifications and training: first aid and CPR certificates, childcare or education qualifications and other training.
  • Background check results, where legally permitted: for example, the Spanish certificate from the Central Register of Sex Offenders (Certificado negativo del Registro Central de Delincuentes Sexuales), which is required by Spanish law for work involving regular contact with minors (article 13.5 of Ley Orgánica 1/1996 and article 57 of Ley Orgánica 8/2021), or an equivalent certificate from your country of nationality or previous residence. We process this information only as authorised by law (article 10 GDPR). [Lawyer to confirm exactly which checks are legally required or permitted, for which nannies, and how results are stored.]
  • Profile content: profile photo, introduction video, a description in your own words and the verification badges you have earned.
  • Work and payment information: bookings, hours, cancellations, bank details, tax and Social Security details as required by your working arrangement, and invoices.
  • Performance information: reviews and feedback from families, complaints, incident reports and our notes on professional standards.

Each verification badge on a nanny profile reflects a check we have actually completed. We keep records showing which checks were done and when.

7. Who can see what: profile visibility

We separate information into levels, so that sensitive information is never automatically visible to everyone.

Public website

Very limited information, such as a nanny's first name, photo and short description, only where the nanny has agreed. No children's details, ever.

Before a booking is confirmed

Families proposed a match may see the nanny's first name, profile photo, introduction video, experience, languages, qualifications, verification badges, reviews, general location and availability. Nannies considering a booking may see the parent's first name, the family introduction (and photo or video, if provided), the number and ages of children, their interests, the general booking location, dates, hours and responsibilities. Phone numbers, email addresses, surnames where appropriate, exact addresses and social media accounts are not shared at this stage.

After a booking is confirmed

Once both sides have accepted and payment is secured, the family and nanny receive the details needed for the booking: contact details, the exact address, the childcare booking brief and relevant allergy or medical information.

Private company records

Passport/ID copies, background-check documents, full reference information, bank details and sensitive health records are kept privately, with access limited to staff who need them for their role. They never appear on a profile.

11. Your rights and how to use them

Under the GDPR and the LOPDGDD you have the right to:

  • Access: find out whether we hold data about you and get a copy.
  • Rectification: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data, where we no longer need it or have no legal basis to keep it.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data, for example while we check its accuracy.
  • Portability: receive data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format, or have it sent to another organisation.
  • Objection: object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Parents and legal guardians exercise these rights on behalf of their children.

How to exercise your rights: email [Data Protection contact] or [Email: cameron@protravelnannies.com], or write to [Registered address], with the subject "Data protection request". If we cannot be sure of your identity, we may ask for information to confirm it. We will reply within one month. This can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you why. Exercising your rights is free, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

12. Right to complain to the AEPD

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid, www.aepd.es. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please contact us.

13. How we keep data secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • keeping general enquiry data, booking data, sensitive child information and verification documents separate, so that sensitive details are not stored in general notes;
  • limiting access according to each person's role, with strong passwords and two-factor authentication;
  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) and reputable providers with their own security certifications;
  • confidentiality obligations for our team and nannies; and
  • a data breach procedure: if a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the AEPD within 72 hours where required (article 33 GDPR), and tell you without undue delay if the risk is high (article 34 GDPR).

No system is completely secure, but we work hard to protect the information you share with us.
