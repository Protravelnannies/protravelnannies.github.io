This policy explains how Pro Travel Nannies uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, in accordance with article 22.2 of Spain's Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services (LSSI-CE), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Spanish Data Protection Agency's guide on the use of cookies (Guía sobre el uso de las cookies, AEPD).

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit it. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences, or to collect statistics about how the site is used.

The same rules apply to similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage (localStorage), pixels and device fingerprinting. In this policy, "cookies" includes all of these.

Cookies can be:

  • First-party (set by this website) or third-party (set by another company's service used on this website);
  • Session (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (kept until they expire or you delete them).

3. Types of cookies

Technical / essential cookies

These are needed for the website to work or to provide a service you have asked for, such as remembering your cookie choice, keeping a form secure or loading pages correctly. Under article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE they do not require your consent, but we still tell you about them.

Preference (personalisation) cookies

These remember choices you make, such as language or region, to give you a more personal experience. Where they are not strictly necessary for a service you requested, we ask for your consent.

Analytics (measurement) cookies

These help us understand how visitors use the website (for example, which pages are visited most), so we can improve it. We only use them with your consent.

Marketing (advertising) cookies

These track browsing to show relevant advertising or measure advertising campaigns. We do not currently use any marketing cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

6. Your consent

When you first visit the website, a short notice explains that we only use essential storage. Because essential storage does not require consent, there is nothing to accept or reject at the moment. If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, the notice will ask for your consent before any of them are set, with "Accept" and "Reject" options that are equally easy to choose. Simply continuing to browse will never be treated as consent to non-essential cookies.

8. Browser controls

You can also block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies and storage may stop some parts of the website from working properly (for example, the banner may appear on every visit). Instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies / Site settings (support.google.com/chrome)
  • Safari (Mac, iPhone, iPad): Settings > Privacy / Manage Website Data (support.apple.com)
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data (support.mozilla.org)
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions (support.microsoft.com)

Many browsers also offer a private or incognito mode, which deletes cookies and site data when you close the window.
