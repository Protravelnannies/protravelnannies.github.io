Booking policies

Booking & cancellation, in plain English.

Clear terms protect everyone: families, nannies and, most importantly, children. Here's how bookings, payments, cancellations, changes and the unexpected are handled, written so you don't need a lawyer to understand them.

Our approach is simple: the goal is not to favour the family or the nanny. It's to create fair protection for both sides, so that everyone knows what happens before they say yes.

1. Confirming a booking

Sending an enquiry, or telling us you're interested, doesn't create a booking. A booking becomes confirmed only when all of the following are true:

  1. The booking details (dates, hours, location, children and responsibilities) have been agreed.
  2. The nanny has accepted the booking.
  3. The family has accepted the match.
  4. The required payment or deposit has been received.
  5. The applicable terms have been accepted.

Only then is the nanny's calendar blocked for you, and only then are contact details released so the family and nanny can communicate directly. Every confirmed booking has a shared childcare brief that both sides can see, so there's one clear record of what was agreed.

2. Payments

Families pay through Pro Travel Nannies, not directly to the nanny. In short:

  • Before you pay, you'll see the full breakdown: childcare cost, any service fee, any travel costs, any additional charges and the total due.
  • Your payment secures the booking. The booking is confirmed once the required payment or deposit is received.
  • After the booking, hours and any agreed changes (such as extra time) are confirmed, and the nanny is paid according to their agreed terms.

Handling payment through us means nannies aren't left chasing money and families have a clear record of what they've paid for.

3. Family cancellations

Plans change, and we understand that. But once a booking is confirmed, your nanny has reserved that time for you and may have turned down other work. Our cancellation terms therefore follow a tiered principle:

When you cancelWhat to expect
Well in advanceFree or low-cost cancellation
Closer to the bookingA partial cancellation charge
Late cancellationA higher cancellation charge
Same day or very lateStronger protection for the nanny's reserved time

Longer holiday nanny and travel nanny bookings have separate cancellation rules, because the nanny may be reserving several days or weeks for your family. Wedding and event bookings may also have their own terms.

The exact time periods and amounts are being finalised with our legal advisers. The terms for your booking will be shown clearly before you pay, so you'll never be surprised.

4. Nanny cancellations & replacement assistance

Nannies make a commitment when they accept a confirmed booking, and we take that seriously. Repeated or unjustified cancellations affect a nanny's standing in our network.

Genuine emergencies do happen, though: illness, a family emergency, an accident or serious transport disruption. When they do:

  • We'll let you know as soon as we can.
  • We'll immediately start looking for another suitable verified nanny, matched as closely as possible to your booking: your children's ages, the location, languages, qualifications and required skills.
  • We'll introduce the replacement to you and update your booking brief.

We'll make every reasonable effort to find a suitable replacement. We can't promise one will always be available, particularly at very short notice, and if we're unable to, we'll talk you through your options under your booking terms.

5. Sick child policy

This policy exists because I've lived the alternative. As a nanny, I once arrived at a booking where a child was very unwell and vomiting, and was asked to leave and come back later, with nothing agreed about what should happen next. Nobody should have to work that out in a stressful moment.

The detailed illness policy is part of our pre-launch professional review. These are the principles it's built on.

Telling us before the booking

Please let us know as soon as possible if your child is unwell before a booking, including:

  • Vomiting or diarrhoea
  • A fever
  • A highly contagious illness
  • A significant respiratory illness
  • Anything else that materially changes the care your child needs

Your nanny should never arrive expecting standard babysitting and find that the booking has become sick-child care. With notice, we can talk through the options with you, and the nanny may choose whether they're comfortable continuing.

Vomiting and diarrhoea

Because these illnesses spread easily, we'll usually recommend postponing care until your child has been symptom-free for a reasonable period. We'll agree the best approach with you and the nanny.

Fever

A child with a fever often needs a parent close by. Whether care can go ahead depends on how unwell your child is and the nanny's comfort; your nanny will need clear instructions and any medication guidance in writing.

Contagious illness

For highly contagious conditions, the nanny may decline the booking. We'll discuss the options with you, including postponing.

If symptoms start during the booking

Your nanny will keep your child safe and comfortable and contact you straight away. If your child is seriously unwell, the nanny will follow our emergency procedure and seek medical help where needed.

When parents need to return

If your child becomes significantly unwell during a booking, for example with repeated vomiting, a high temperature or a condition that needs a parent's decision, we'll ask you to return as soon as reasonably possible.

If the nanny has already travelled

If a nanny has already travelled to the booking and care can't go ahead because of illness, the nanny's time and travel have still been committed. How this is handled, including what remains payable, will be set out in the final terms shown before you pay.

Nannies are never pressured to provide care that falls outside the agreed booking or creates an unreasonable health or safety risk.

6. Changes to a confirmed booking

Small updates, like a slightly different dinner time, are easy. But significant changes need the nanny's agreement, because they change what the nanny accepted. These include:

  • Adding more children
  • Changing the location or accommodation
  • Extending the hours or changing the dates
  • Adding driving responsibilities
  • Adding swimming supervision
  • Turning an evening booking into overnight care
  • Adding household responsibilities
  • Adding significant travel

Please contact us about any change before the booking begins. Material changes may change the price, and the nanny can accept or decline them. If they decline, we'll help you find another solution.

7. Additional hours

Every booking has an agreed start and finish time. If you'd like your nanny to stay longer:

  1. Ask your nanny, as early as you can. They'll agree where reasonably possible.
  2. The additional time is recorded.
  3. You're charged at the agreed additional-hours rate.
  4. Your nanny is paid for the extra time.

Please don't assume a nanny is automatically available beyond the agreed finish time; they may have other commitments or a booking the next morning.

8. Travel costs

Any travel costs are agreed and shown before confirmation. Depending on the booking, they may include taxis (including a safe late-night taxi home), public transport, mileage, parking, ferries, trains, flights, airport transfers, accommodation and meals. They're most common for holiday and travel nannies, weddings and events, remote villas and late-night babysitting. A nanny should never have to personally absorb reasonable costs needed to do the booking.

9. Holiday & travel nanny bookings

Longer bookings receive a more detailed personal review before confirmation, and the booking agreement clearly covers:

  • Working hours, breaks and days off
  • Accommodation, sleeping arrangements and a private room where applicable
  • Meals, flights and local transport
  • Overnight responsibilities and additional hours
  • Expenses and any responsibilities beyond childcare
  • Changes to the itinerary
  • Travel documents for international trips
  • Cancellation terms specific to the booking

10. Booking through us (off-platform bookings)

When we introduce a family and a nanny, all childcare resulting from that introduction should continue to be booked through Pro Travel Nannies for a defined period. The exact scope and duration will be set out in our final terms.

This isn't about making communication difficult. Booking through us keeps verification, secure payment, cancellation protection, support, replacement assistance and a clear booking record in place for both of you. And if your family would like to hire your nanny privately on a long-term basis, please talk to us: we plan to offer an official private-hire route so this can happen openly and fairly.

11. Disputes

If something goes wrong, contact us. Our principle is: Protect the child first. Treat the family fairly. Treat the nanny fairly. Review the facts.

  1. The issue is reported to us.
  2. We review the booking brief and any relevant information.
  3. We contact both the family and the nanny.
  4. We review the facts, without assuming either side is right.
  5. We resolve the matter according to the agreed terms and keep a confidential record.

Anything involving a child's safety is treated as a priority. Read more on our Trust & Safety page.

12. Feedback

After every booking, both sides are invited to share feedback. Families can tell us about the nanny's reliability, communication and care; nannies can tell us about communication, the accuracy of the booking information and their overall experience. Feedback helps us recognise excellent nannies and families, improve matching and spot anything that needs attention. Not all feedback is published, and we have clear rules to prevent unfair or retaliatory reviews.
